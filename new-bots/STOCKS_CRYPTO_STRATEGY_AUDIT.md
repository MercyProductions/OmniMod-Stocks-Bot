# Stocks and Crypto strategy separation audit

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-30 against the current `stocks-bot` and `crypto-bot` source, configuration examples, existing audits, and tests. This is a local code audit. It does not verify production configuration, provider entitlements, deployed workers, account-level rate limits, or realized trading performance.

## Conclusion

The two bots already have distinct short-term and longer-horizon research paths. Their core scores are not mixed into a single stock/crypto “investment score,” their outcome journals are separate, and Discord titles identify tactical calls as short-term paper trades. The highest priority is therefore not another indicator or a lower entry threshold. It is to make the limits of longer-horizon research unmistakable, collect clean prospective evidence, and avoid describing technical trend screens as complete fundamental investment analysis.

No strategy weight, alert threshold, position limit, or model approval rule was changed during this audit. Existing paper and shadow controls remain in place.

## Coverage and data limits

- Stocks are configured for up to 75 symbols plus up to 15 dynamic discoveries per scan (90 before context inputs); the actual provider-returned universe and scan counts are unknown without a credentialed Alpaca run. Dynamic discovery only changes the production candidate pool when the relevant feed/account works. Its downstream strategy gates remain in place.
- Crypto is capped at market-cap rank 250, an 8-asset deep-provider check by default, and 5 tactical signals. The provider-returned unique asset count and exchange coverage are unknown without a live provider observation. This is broad discovery relative to a tiny watchlist, but not the entire token market.
- Stock longer-horizon financial evidence is based on available SEC submissions/XBRL facts and market history. It does not yet establish durable competitive advantage, forward earnings quality, a full dilution history, fair value, or forecast valuation. Current estimates and ratios are explicitly backward-looking and data-limited.
- Crypto longer-horizon analysis lacks complete point-in-time unlock/vesting schedules, holder concentration, chain-specific usage/revenue, exploit/bridge risk history, and token-identity assurance for all assets. Some protocol/supply research exists separately, but coverage and historical depth are not sufficient for a comprehensive investment thesis.
- Catalyst coverage is asymmetric. Stock event gates cover earnings, selected filings/corporate actions, macro context, and available calendars; optional Alpaca company headlines receive conservative display/research-only topic hints, but this does not establish exhaustive real-time company-news coverage. Crypto has manual classified events, optional US macro blocks, and optional market headlines with conservative keyword topic tags for obvious exchange, security, regulatory, supply, and network events. Tags do not verify the story, infer actual price impact or alter approval. Coverage remains optional and incomplete; it does not provide comprehensive exchange/token/chain incident, unlock, listing, or delisting monitoring.
- Historical examples are not special-case forecast rules. Regime features use observed price, breadth, volatility, and available macro context. They do not currently classify every credit, geopolitical, liquidity, or policy regime listed in the user framework. Unknown catalyst or regime coverage must remain unknown, not neutral evidence.

## What works and what remains unproven

**Works in code:** the tactical and longer-horizon computations, outcomes, and presentation are separate; quote freshness, stale/missing data, cost, exposure, stop, emergency stop, and model abstention controls are tested; no-trade is a valid output; provider and missing-data caveats are visible; and deterministic analytics are implemented in code rather than delegated to an AI score.

**Not proven:** positive expectancy, calibration quality under independent market days, resilience across historical crisis regimes, full alert recall, current deployed alert frequency, complete provider coverage, comprehensive fundamentals/tokenomics, and performance of the dynamic stock/crypto universes. Automated fixtures do not answer those empirical questions. The Crypto model audit requires a larger prospective shadow set and later-created champion comparisons; cross-asset OHLCV exploration is not a blind validation of the production strategy. Stocks similarly lacks an adequate licensed point-in-time market dataset and prospective cohort here.

**Potentially weak or redundant evidence:** stock moving averages, momentum, breakout, VWAP, relative-strength, and regime features are correlated representations of price trend; crypto return momentum, technical oscillators, derivatives and order-flow may share information. Feature weights are heuristic/model inputs, not causal contributions. There is no source-grounded ablation proving each feature adds out-of-sample value. Do not remove or increase these weights until preregistered ablations with later holdouts exist.

**Alert frequency:** configured caps are 5 signals per scan/day as applicable; no local production history is available to say whether deployed alerts are too frequent or restrictive. A cap or low count is not a performance metric.

## Prioritized work and acceptance metrics

1. **Keep boundaries explicit (safe; no strategy change).** Preserve `SHORT-TERM PAPER TRADE`, `SWING-HORIZON DAILY TREND RESEARCH`, and `DAILY PRICE-TREND WATCHLIST` labels. Continue using separate prediction/outcome and investment journals. Acceptance: regression tests ensure each publication route and summary uses the correct journal and horizon label, and that daily ranks are never presented as calibrated probability or a buy/hold instruction.
2. **Measure funnel and provider health (safe infrastructure).** Capture source mode, unique assets, screened, deep-checked, rejected-by-reason, model approval/abstention, risk approval, alert count, request attempts, latency, 429/retry counts, returned rate-limit headers, and scan duration. Report provider limits as unknown when not returned. Acceptance: one credentialed or explicitly unavailable scan per bot produces a complete stage record with unknown distinct from observed zero.
3. **Improve long-horizon evidence coverage (strategy-adjacent; shadow first).** Add only source-backed, timestamped fundamentals/tokenomics/catalysts; retain observed-versus-missing status and no-score defaults. Acceptance: point-in-time coverage audits, source-level freshness/revision metadata, and frozen feature ablations with sufficient independent dates. No feature becomes an approval gate or changes a score before paper validation.
4. **Test regimes and correlated features (strategy change; preregistration required).** Compare the frozen production score to one-factor-removed and regime-stratified shadow cohorts using point-in-time data, costed fills, multiple-testing control, distinct-session coverage, expectancy, drawdown, Brier/calibration and adverse/favorable excursion. Keep champion unchanged until later-blind evidence beats it.
5. **Accumulate realistic evidence.** Stocks require licensed point-in-time, survivorship-aware histories and actual consolidated executable quotes/cost schedules. Crypto requires timestamped venue quotes/books and fee tiers; OHLCV-only tests cannot establish fills. Promotion needs untouched out-of-sample and multi-session forward-paper cohorts, not activity or win rate alone.

The next implementation phase is per-run end-to-end funnel instrumentation for Stocks and Crypto, reconciled to current interfaces without loosening strategy or risk gates. Provider-authenticated counts, real trading performance, and any resulting strategy promotion remain explicitly unverified.
