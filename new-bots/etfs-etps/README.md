This is a separate, paper-only ETF/ETP project. Its initial strategy is a 15-minute opening-range breakout: it requires continuous completed five-minute bars from the session open, a close beyond the opening range plus a volatility/price buffer, matching VWAP direction, at least 1.5× trailing relative volume, and a synchronized fresh executable bid/ask. It sets the stop at the opening-range midpoint and the initial target at 2R. It rejects a new entry during the final 30 minutes, during a halt or configured catalyst blackout, after a data gap, or when the stop exceeds the configured maximum distance. Strategy overrides are explicitly bounded in `src/strategies.js`; they may tighten, but not loosen, the default gates.

The 15-minute opening range requires at least 24 completed same-session bars by default. Maximum quote age is 15 seconds and quote/bar skew is capped at 90 seconds. Overrides cannot lower relative volume below 1.5×, reduce the volatility/price breakout buffers, raise maximum stop distance above 1.25%, reduce minimum reward/risk below 1.5R or target reward/risk below 2R, or shorten the 30-minute pre-close entry cutoff. Larger ranges, higher relative-volume requirements, wider buffers, tighter stops, better reward/risk, and earlier cutoff times remain valid stricter variants.

Candidate quote, OHLCV, ETP leverage, and time-of-day volume fields must be finite numbers or nonblank numeric strings. Malformed bars and quote fields are rejected; invalid historical volume observations cannot replace valid slot samples or inflate the relative-volume baseline.

`ETF_SYMBOLS` is a priority list in the default `ETF_DISCOVERY_MODE=full-market`, not the searchable universe. Each directory member must resolve to an exchange-flagged ETF or fresh source-reviewed ETP classification and a unique active/tradable Alpaca equity identity before cheap quote requests. Alpaca's active `us_equity` listing alone does not prove that a symbol is an ETF. Unknown priority symbols are reported; ETNs and other wrappers still need product-type, exposure, leverage and source review. Explicit `ETF_DISCOVERY_MODE=baseline` retains the old configured/mover selection solely for comparisons.

Run `npm test` to verify strategy and provider behavior. `npm run scan` defaults to `ETF_SOURCE=fixture` and reads `ETF_SESSION_FILE` (`examples/paper-session.json`). Set `ETF_SOURCE=alpaca` and credentials for authenticated research. Full-market mode refreshes the two Nasdaq Trader ETF directories, records membership additions/removals/changes, and checks every member against Alpaca's active asset master. All eligible members receive inexpensive [bulk snapshots](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stocksnapshots-1) in batches of at most 100, with at least 350ms between batch starts and existing retry/429 controls. Every eligible symbol is considered before the 1–200 `ETF_DISCOVERY_MAX_CANDIDATES` deep-analysis budget. Cheap ranking uses current price movement, session range, gap and dollar volume; configured priorities only break equal ranks. This is a workload heuristic, not a probability or new trading strategy. Only shortlisted symbols proceed to unchanged catalyst checks, news, refreshed quotes and intraday history. An auth/entitlement/quota failure defers remaining batches and deep work; other partial failures and missing symbols remain explicit.

Directory metadata refreshes every six hours with a reported fallback up to 48 hours old. They are not a complete ETP security master: products without ETF flags or reviewed supplemental identity are still a coverage gap. Each full screen archives per-symbol identity/data exclusions and budget deferrals as compressed JSON in `ETF_DISCOVERY_HISTORY_PATH` (default `.cache/etf-discovery-history`), retained for 14 days. Scan/operator reports contain compact counts, quota/latency evidence, archive filename and membership-change samples. The Alpaca path checks the official market clock and calendar, checks the full directory against the active/tradable asset master, cheaply screens bulk snapshots and downloads five-minute bars only for shortlisted, catalyst-cleared symbols, and rejects stale quotes and delayed SIP entry data. Inactive, non-tradable, and unsupported-listing symbols are omitted before quote requests. IEX data is screening-only; only the SIP consolidated feed can carry executable quote quality into paper candidate qualification. Provider mode requires a fresh per-symbol catalyst review from `ETF_CATALYST_REVIEW_FILE` (default `examples/catalyst-reviews.json`) with `{ "SPY": { "status": "clear", "source": "...", "summary": "...", "reviewedAt": "ISO timestamp" } }`. The sample file is empty, so no ETF passes that gate until it is reviewed. Symbols with missing or stale reviews are rejected before requesting news, reducing unnecessary calls. For reviewed symbols, recent ticker-linked Alpaca News records preserve separate article creation and update timestamps and invalidate a review when either timestamp is newer than its review time; optional `ETF_CATALYST_SOURCE=finnhub` adds a conservative US macro-event blackout but does not replace ticker reviews or headline checks; news-provider or pagination failures fail closed. Review remains manual. Automated macro classification and quantitative leveraged/inverse reset, commodity-roll, ETN issuer-credit, distribution, and corporate-action risk modeling are not integrated; alerts only disclose classified wrapper risk categories. Account entitlements and feed coverage depend on Alpaca configuration.

The default `ETF_ETP_CLASSIFICATION_PATH` points to `examples/etp-classifications.json`, which records current, issuer-cited daily leverage terms for leveraged/inverse symbols in the curated seed list and known shared-exposure aliases, plus the DJP unleveraged commodity-index ETN with SEC filing provenance. DJP is labeled with the broad `COMMODITY_BASKET` exposure key; its current listing/tradability must still pass Alpaca's asset-master check. SEC filing disclosures identify DJP as an unsecured Barclays ETN linked to a commodity-futures index, so its issuer-credit and index/roll risks are distinct from owning commodities directly and are not modeled by this bot ([SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/312070/000031207024000013/a221bbplc-2023x12x31x20fxex.htm)). Refresh the citations and `reviewedAt` values at least every 180 days. To add an ETN, ETV, commodity pool, or other wrapper, add a reviewed JSON row with `symbol`, `productType` (`etn`, `etv`, `commodity_pool`, `leveraged_etf`, or `inverse_etf`), `exposureKey`, numeric `leverage`, HTTPS `sourceUrl`, and recent `reviewedAt`. Invalid, stale, or incomplete wrapper rows are withheld from discovery (including when an exchange directory separately labels them as an ETF); they are not silently downgraded to ordinary 1x ETFs. Candidate security-master names that contain explicit leverage/inverse markers but lack a fresh source-reviewed classification are also withheld as a conservative reject-only safeguard; a name match never classifies a product or qualifies a candidate. Supplemental entries are still intersected with Alpaca's active/tradable listing and the cheap snapshot screen and must pass all existing catalyst, executable-quote, strategy, and portfolio-risk gates. Declared leverage scales the shared portfolio gross-exposure limit using absolute leverage while stop-risk sizing and paper P&L remain based on the wrapper's own quoted price. Candidate metadata establishes identity and declared exposure. Paper alerts disclose daily-reset/path risk for leveraged/inverse wrappers, issuer-credit risk for ETNs, or futures-roll risk for commodity pools, explicitly labeled not modeled. These tags are not quantitative risk adjustments; source contents are not fetched or independently verified, and liquidity, suitability, distribution, and corporate-action risks remain unmodeled. The supplemental registry is re-read and review dates are checked on every scan, even when the exchange directory is cached; changes do not require deleting the directory cache. Only explicit `ETF_DISCOVERY_MODE=baseline` retains the old `ETF_SYMBOLS` universe override for comparisons; matching reviewed metadata and stale-wrapper rejection still apply. The seeded `NVDQ` alias has been corrected to current GraniteShares ticker `NVD`; confirm active/tradable status with Alpaca before a setup can be considered.

Each `trades` row passed to the strategy includes `symbol`, optional `assetClass` (`etf` or `etp`), `exposureKey`, completed five-minute OHLCV `bars`, an executable `quote` (`bid`, `ask`, `time`, `quality: "executable"`), and a session (`marketDate`, `openTime`, `closeTime`, plus optional halt/catalyst flags). Root `sessionDate`, or a trade's market date, groups daily performance. When no explicit session, trade, or mark date is present, macro-calendar queries and daily P&L summaries fall back to the America/New_York local date. The runner canonicalizes known wrapper risk: QQQ/TQQQ/SQQQ, SPY/VOO/UPRO/SPXU, semiconductor and biotech leveraged pairs, NVDA leveraged products, bond-duration pairs, and BTC/ETH-linked ETPs share an underlying exposure key. DJP, DBC, and PDBC retain their primary identities and also intersect mapped gold, silver, oil, natural-gas, agriculture, and CME grain/oilseed/livestock exposure buckets; DBA has a distinct agriculture key and intersects mapped agricultural futures. These are conservative identity checks, not changing-weight or correlation models, and do not cover every commodity held by the funds. Scope references: [Invesco DBC](https://www.invesco.com/us/en/financial-products/etfs/invesco-db-commodity-index-tracking-fund.html), [Invesco PDBC](https://www.invesco.com/us-rest/contentdetail?contentId=03b8588c0971b410VgnVCM100000c2f1bf0aRCRD), [Invesco DBA fact sheet](https://www.invesco.com/content/dam/invesco/us/en/product-documents/etf/fact-sheet/dba-invesco-db-agriculture-fund-fact-sheet.pdf), and [CME agricultural contract codes](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/faqs/faq-micro-agriculture-futures.html). BTC and ETH aliases match the Crypto primary-signal convention; SPY and QQQ aliases align to the Futures equity-index keys. Unknown symbols use their ticker or an explicit `exposureKey` from the input. Manual `assetClass: "etp"` candidates must additionally include a recognized `productType`, explicit canonical `exposureKey`, consistent leverage/inverse values, an HTTPS source URL, and a classification review no older than 180 days; missing or stale classifications fail before strategy approval. Source-reviewed leverage metadata scales gross-exposure accounting for supplemental wrapper rows; it does not change stop-risk units or simulate the product's reset path. A `marks` array can advance existing orders using `{ orderId, quote: { bid, ask, time, quality: "executable" }, sessionOpen, sessionDate }`; only a later fresh quote can fill a reservation. Stop gaps exit at the observed adverse quote; targets use the target limit; forced session exits use the current executable side.

Risk approval requires a positive `PAPER_ACCOUNT_EQUITY_USD`, explicitly configured fee/slippage reserves, a fresh executable quote, configured risk limits, and `OMNIMOD_EMERGENCY_STOP=false`. Blank cost settings block candidate creation; do not treat an assumed zero-cost plan as validated. The stop is active by default. The local ledger records later quote-based paper fills/marks/exits, costs, realized/unrealized P&L, daily loss, and summary performance metrics. This is a quote-event simulator, not a calibrated market-impact/fill model. Alpaca linked-company headlines and an optional Finnhub scheduled-event blackout are implemented, but coverage/entitlement is unverified. `npm run backtest -- <dataset.json>` replays caller-supplied chronological bars, executable quotes, sessions, reviews, and explicit costs; it does not download historical data or establish out-of-sample strategy performance. Add an optional `evaluationWindows` array of 2–20 `{ id, start, end, resolutionEnd? }` timestamp rows to score a frozen strategy on separate chronological windows. `start`, `end`, and optional `resolutionEnd` must be ISO-8601 timestamps with explicit UTC offsets. `end` is the last time new strategy decisions may be made. A later `resolutionEnd` can provide quotes to fill/manage/close paper orders generated by decisions inside the scored window, but no evaluator calls occur after `end`. Set that buffer long enough for the strategy's maximum order lifetime and trade horizon; any still-open order at `resolutionEnd` remains unresolved rather than being silently graded. The next window must start strictly after the preceding `resolutionEnd` to preserve an embargo. If omitted, `resolutionEnd` equals `end`, retaining legacy behavior. This format is also supported by Forex, Futures, and Options replay CLIs. Each window gets a fresh paper ledger; earlier observations are warm-up only. These windows are genuine untouched holdouts only if dates and strategy were fixed before outcomes were inspected. No brokerage connection or order routing is implemented.

Stocks and Crypto publish successfully delivered, tracked directional calls to a shared append-only research journal (`OMNIMOD_SIGNAL_RESEARCH_PATH`, default `new-bots/shared/data/primary-signals.jsonl`). The ETF/ETP runner reads recent Stocks/Crypto records (five-minute default; configure `ETF_PRIMARY_SIGNAL_MAX_AGE_MS`) and attaches direction, source bot, time, and canonical exposure matches to scan results and alerts as **advisory context only**. It never uses a journal row as an ETF entry or to bypass the ETF's own session, catalyst, bars, executable quote, strategy, and shared-risk gates. If the journal cannot be read, the ETF scan continues without shared context and reports the read error. Quote-driven fills remain simplified paper simulations; no performance or profitability claim is made.

ETF scan diagnostics combine provider discovery stages (configured universe, active/tradable securities, catalyst clearance, scanned symbols, and usable market data) with strategy candidates, strategy rejections, portfolio-risk rejections, reservations, provider failures, ranked rejection reasons, per-provider latency, and elapsed time. Alpaca request failures are retained and journaled as a failed scan; provider configuration errors still stop startup. Alpaca quota headers and utilization are reported when returned; missing headers remain unknown. Full-market discovery exposes returned/usable snapshot counts independently of catalyst-cleared detailed strategy inputs.
