## What this establishes

- The project does not have one shared all-market universe. Stocks, Crypto, Forex, Futures and ETFs have discovery stages; Options is a downstream signal consumer. Forex discovery is account-specific and gated on OANDA practice configuration.
- Options has the clearest discovery gap: the current strategy consumes fresh primary Stocks/ETF-ETP signals or manual symbols, and Tradier chains require a specific underlying plus expiration. Its partial text-search endpoint is not a broad option opportunity screener. This is a provider-capability boundary, not a failed scan; do not brute-force search queries. A broader Options scan needs a cost-bounded underlying shortlist from an existing primary scanner or a separately verified broad discovery capability.
- Forex has two configured fallback pairs, but its default-enabled OANDA dynamic discovery can select a wider account-supported G10 shortlist up to 20 total when practice mode and credentials are configured. Since this checkout lacks those credentials, the effective runtime universe currently remains disabled (0 provider-discovered pairs), and no account-specific count can be claimed. Expanding the deep-scan cap would multiply four-timeframe candle requests per added pair; first collect account-backed quota and latency measurements, without changing strategy/risk gates.
- Stocks' provider universe may contain thousands of active eligible equities, but each run intentionally deep-screens a small number. Increasing that cap would change request volume and execution/data cost; no strategy thresholds were changed in this audit.
- ETFs' nominal 200-scan cap is not proof that 200 instruments are actually discovered or scanned; exact rows require a successful current directory and screener response.
