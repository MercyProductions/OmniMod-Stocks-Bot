## Read this first

The six primary owners are `stocks-bot`, `crypto-bot`, and `new-bots/{futures,forex,options,etfs-etps}`. Stocks and Crypto are mature Discord applications with their own commands, guild settings, schedulers, data stores, prediction journals, and day versus longer-horizon research. The other four are separate Node runners that can perform one scan or run under the shared scheduler; they use Discord webhooks. Supplemental `new-bots/{multi-asset,prediction-markets,defi-yield,onchain-inefficiencies}` are read-only research and do not own an instrument in the six-bot paper ledger.

Each owner has a deterministic market-specific day strategy. The shared `new-bots/shared/paperRisk.js` is the final paper gate and atomic reservation ledger: it checks primary owner, fresh executable bid/ask, market session, costs, position sizing, maximum account risk/notional/margin, duplicate instrument/exposure, currency conflicts, marked open positions, daily loss/circuit rules, and emergency stop. `exposureAliases.js` links some inverse wrappers, options underlyings, commodities, and Treasury futures/ETPs. It does **not** calculate statistical cross-market correlations or fully weighted basket overlap. There is no broker/exchange order route. “Fill” means a simulated paper fill using sampled quotes, not a real execution.

The read-only operator portfolio summary additionally groups active paper risk/notional by declared asset class and by recorded effective underlying long/short direction. An inverse wrapper or option can differ from its purchased instrument direction. Unknown class/direction and missing risk/notional stay explicit; no delta equivalence, constituent weights, correlation, or hedge credit is inferred. These fields do not change the shared approval rules. The local ledger currently has no active positions with which to validate real concentration patterns.

The unified research dashboard now carries those open-exposure groups into JSON and self-contained HTML beside closed-trade performance. When the local ledger file is absent it reports open risk/notional as unavailable, not as a verified zero. Focused and full six-market checks passed, and a real local read-only report produced that missing-ledger state. This makes portfolio visibility easier to audit; it does not supply the missing provider-backed positions or a quantitative hedge/correlation estimate.

The ledger summary, operator report, and research dashboard share `closedTradeSequence.js` for descriptive maximum/current closed-trade drawdown and consecutive-loss streaks. These metrics require complete net P&L and uniquely ordered close timestamps; otherwise they are null with a coverage/status reason. Shared ledger/operator portfolio totals also leave net/open P&L unavailable when a required closed result or open mark is missing; the dashboard withholds whole-cohort net metrics on missing close coverage. An unknown UTC-day total fails the next paper approval rather than becoming zero, including in forward-shadow comparison. Optional `OMNIMOD_MAX_CONSECUTIVE_LOSSES` and `OMNIMOD_MAX_CLOSED_DRAWDOWN_PCT` now enforce paper-only portfolio circuits at the atomic reservation lock. They are unset in this checkout pending operator threshold review. Once configured, missing/ambiguous close chronology fails closed and the first successful configured reservation anchors matching policy values for all six bot processes on that ledger. Maximum historical closed-trade drawdown remains breached after a later recovery. Open/intratrade drawdown is outside this measure; daily-loss, fresh open marks, and emergency-stop gates remain separate. See `PROVIDER_SETUP_CHECKLIST.md` before configuring these variables.

`primarySignal.js` publishes at most three displayed **reserved paper setups** per owner to the shared journal, and only after matching Discord delivery succeeds. Fresh peer rows are advisory context, not executable quotes. Options can consume Stocks and ETF/ETP directional rows, and conflicting directions are withheld. Shared functions include `signalResearch.js`, `forwardShadowPaper.js`, `chronologicalReplay.js`, `scheduler.js`, diagnostics, and `operatorReport.js`. Paper records and shadow strategy states remain separated by bot/strategy.

Every bot currently has a default-active emergency stop and no live order path. The project has not completed account-authenticated scans, multi-session forward paper evaluation, or point-in-time out-of-sample evaluation for all six markets. Do not turn on live trading or claim performance from fixture tests.

This is source-reported evidence, not proof of the entire upstream decision path. The journal binds each assessment to the terminal order fingerprint, rejects malformed or stale revision chains, and has no authority over strategy approval or order routing; see `PAPER_TRADE_REVIEW.md`. Exclusions carry reasons. Even an eligible ordering is descriptive, not evidence of a superior strategy or a promotion decision; the local ledger currently has no qualifying cohort.

Forward-shadow tracks declare a `strategyVersion`, hypothesis, change, and reason. A write-once per-experiment manifest freezes a 60-day forward observation window; the creating scan cannot reserve its own candidates. The runner separates candidate/outcome metrics by version/configuration hash and excludes pre-freeze rows. An edited/malformed manifest, expired window, or same-hash legacy cohort with no manifest blocks new shadow reservations while prior open positions continue to receive marks. If an override changes, it still discovers and marks the prior profile ledger; a prior open position or same-UTC-day close withholds replacement shadow entries. All six shadow versions were bumped for this manifest boundary. Bump them again whenever source semantics change without a base-configuration change. The historical and walk-forward result fields are explicitly unavailable and the decision stays pending manual review; no forward performance exists here.

The descriptive day-cluster expectancy interval is unavailable if a required closed record has missing/invalid session, unreconciled costs, P&L, or initial risk.
