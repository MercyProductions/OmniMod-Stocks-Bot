## Independent costed paper research (local implementation, 2026-10-03)

Independent technical watches can now feed `independent-orb-debit-vertical-v3`, an **unvalidated, isolated forward-paper experiment**. No Stocks/ETF alert is required. This does not promote the underlying hypothesis into production or send approved entry alerts. Production and independent research counts remain separate in the runner, scan journal, operator report and `/options scanstats`.

The new bridge preserves the original underlying target/invalidation and reconfirms the breakout using a fresh Tradier underlying quote after chain retrieval. It then applies the existing standard-contract identity, synchronized quote, liquidity, spread, expiry, catalyst, call-dividend/assignment review, cost and reward/risk checks to a defined-risk debit vertical. A target already passed is not moved further away to manufacture a new setup. Missing fees/slippage or required review data blocks new research candidates. Optional Finnhub/Alpaca overlays retain the existing failure and invalidation behavior.

`OPTIONS_INDEPENDENT_PAPER_SOURCE=auto` requires existing Tradier **production market-data** access; `tradier` selects it explicitly and `disabled` preserves the preceding research baseline. This is data access only: no order endpoint exists in this path. Sandbox quotes cannot reserve or fill the experiment. Defaults cap deep spread work at three technical watches and 45 seconds per scan (`OPTIONS_INDEPENDENT_PAPER_MAX_UNDERLYINGS=1..12`, `OPTIONS_INDEPENDENT_PAPER_BUDGET_MS=1000..60000`). The cap follows full eligible-universe screening. Existing discovery profiles determine IEX/SIP underlying research; IEX limitations remain explicit. Provider requests retain pacing, short bounded waits, quota headroom and timeouts.

The experiment lives under `OMNIMOD_FORWARD_SHADOW_STATE_DIR/options-independent` and reads the shared production ledger for exposure and loss checks. It never writes production reservations. Its additional hypothetical losses combine with the actual book for the daily loss gate; mirrored baseline experiments retain their existing behavior. The paper emergency stop defaults on, account equity and costs must be configured, and live trading stays unavailable. Changing feed, strategy/configuration or frozen risk parameters creates a separate cohort. Prior cohorts remain monitored and can prevent replacing unresolved exposure.

The first otherwise valid candidate freezes the experiment manifest; that observation cannot reserve. Subsequent observations must fall inside the frozen forward window, pass shared risk, and use later quotes. A reservation fills only from a still later executable composite quote. Monitoring continues when the underlying leaves the shortlist and while the emergency stop prevents new entries. Missing/stale/invalid marks leave outcomes unresolved. Fees and slippage are deducted, and the existing forward report separates candidates, reservations, fills, completed outcomes, pending orders, excursions, returns, drawdown, expectancy and sample size. Synthetic lifecycle success is not financial performance evidence.

Persistent `observations-YYYY-MM-DD.jsonl` records preserve normalized chain inputs, decisions, diagnostics and experiment references. The original content-addressed underlying archive is checked against the selected observations and copied into `options-independent/underlying-inputs` before evaluation; these frozen copies have no automatic pruning. The ordinary discovery archives retain their separate 14-day policy. The read-only `probe:universe` command does not start this paper experiment or send Discord messages.

## Independent intraday research (local implementation, 2026-10-03)

The independent shortlist now receives bounded minute-bar analysis without waiting for Stocks or ETF alerts. This is a new, **unvalidated research hypothesis**, not a production strategy promotion. `options-underlying-orb-research-v1` and its frozen rule hash identify the cohort; IEX and SIP observations remain separate. Existing approved setups, risk decisions, costs and paper execution are unchanged.

The fixed hypothesis uses a 15-minute opening range, at least 24 completed regular-session minutes, a first close crossing within the last two completed bars, a buffer of the larger of 0.05 prior ATR or 5 basis points, crossing-minute volume of at least 1.5 times the previous 20 minutes, continued price/quote confirmation, and session VWAP alignment. Both directions are evaluated. Underlying reference invalidation is the opening-range midpoint, capped at 1.25% distance; the reference target is 2R. These levels describe the **underlying**, not option premium, an option fill, or a forecast of option returns. The trailing-volume ratio is not time-of-day adjusted. No new observation qualifies in the final 30 minutes of the regular session.

Data checks require positive numeric provider OHLC/volume/VWAP values, continuous aligned minutes from the open, quotes no older than 15 seconds, completed-bar age/quote alignment within 90 seconds, positive bid/ask sizes, spread no wider than 0.2%, and midpoint drift no more than 0.5% from the last close. Missing minutes are rejected, never synthesized; this can materially limit single-venue IEX coverage. Early breakouts before enough completed minutes, events outside regular hours, and moves between scans can be missed. The two-bar observation window is not continuous monitoring or proof of complete breakout capture.

History is batched for up to 12 independently shortlisted identities. Every returned page is consumed before evaluating any symbol; repeated tokens, unexpected symbols and incomplete pagination cannot yield a partial signal. The query freezes the completed-minute cutoff and requests raw same-session prices. Fresh quotes are fetched **after** history using the same explicitly selected feed. A SIP entitlement failure never silently changes to IEX. Requests retain observed quotas, five-request headroom, 350ms minimum start spacing, bounded timeouts, and a default 45-second research budget. This is optional workload after production monitoring/delivery; cross-process quota coordination and continuous quote monitoring remain separate work.

`OPTIONS_INTRADAY_RESEARCH_SOURCE=auto` enables this path with existing Alpaca credentials and a valid independent shortlist; `alpaca` selects it explicitly and `disabled` preserves the prior research baseline. `OPTIONS_INTRADAY_RESEARCH_MAX_UNDERLYINGS` accepts 1–12; `OPTIONS_INTRADAY_RESEARCH_BUDGET_MS` accepts 1,000–60,000. These affect work allocation, not the fixed strategy rules. Free/paid discovery profiles also determine this path's IEX/SIP feed. No real environment file was changed.

The runner refreshes shared Stocks/ETF context independently of its production signal selector. Fresh opposing directions, including share-class aliases, mark an otherwise qualified observation `withheld_for_conflict`; missing/unverified context is labelled separately. An aligned source never approves the strategy. Research rows are never appended to production signals or published as approved Discord entries. Only the separate bridge described above can evaluate them for isolated research reservations after additional checks. Administrator `/options scanstats`, the top-level operator report and scan journals distinguish independent technical watches from production candidates/reservations. Normal embedded status messages include aggregate research counts only.

Full bar pages, quotes, inputs, rules, timestamps and evaluations are archived as immutable content-addressed gzip JSON under `.cache/options-intraday-research` (14-day retention). These operational archives are **not** an option outcome ledger. The separate paper experiment now preserves its selected source archives permanently before evaluating new candidates. The production forward-shadow cohort is unchanged. Use `npm run probe:universe -- --technical --contracts` for read-only underlying, technical and contract diagnostics; this probe does not read primary-signal context, publish Discord messages or reserve orders.

Independent costed paper infrastructure is described above; it does not establish an approved strategy. Synthetic tests establish behavior only; no profitability or operational-market-coverage claim follows.

## Contract identity and paper-result integrity (local implementation, 2026-10-02)

All Options paths now share standard-contract identity validation: OCC root/date/call-put/strike must agree with provider fields and requested underlying; multiplier must explicitly be 100; padded duplicate IDs are rejected. Adjusted roots and unknown deliverables remain visible as unverified. This is a necessary eligibility check, not proof of corporate-action history or fill quality. Malformed/partial payloads are provider failures, not zero-member markets. Bid and ask times are checked separately and must be synchronized.

The day strategy checks normalized/manual legs too. Each leg must supply a real OCC `symbol`, explicit `underlying`, `expiry`, `type`, `strike` and `multiplier`, alongside existing quote/liquidity fields. Placeholder IDs such as `SPY-C100` cannot qualify. Valid spread formulas, thresholds, risk controls and costs are unchanged. Candidates carry identity version `standard-listed-option-v1`, also stored in persisted `option` metadata. Forward cohorts use `underlying-confirmed-debit-vertical-shadow-v6-entry-evidence`; prior results remain separate.

The position monitor verifies the stored spread and returned quote identities before constructing a composite. `option.underlyingSymbol` is the actual underlying; `exposureKey` may be a shared risk group such as `UST_RATES` for TLT. Older complete OCC records can supply their root; missing expiry, strikes or multiplier are never inferred. Invalid legacy records remain unmarked with diagnostics until reviewed. No existing ledger was migrated or deleted.

Replay checks **every observation**, including later fill/exit marks, against the fixed spread and both leg quotes. Invalid marks are unusable and counted in `optionQuoteValidation`; they cannot manufacture a fill, target hit, or closing profit. Missing valid later data leaves positions unresolved. Operator reports expose primary-chain contract counts/reasons in `marketData.contractQuality`, separate from the underlying funnel and independent inventory.

## Independent contract inventory (local implementation, 2026-10-02)

`OPTIONS_CONTRACT_DISCOVERY_SOURCE=auto` uses a configured Tradier token after a valid independent underlying scan. `tradier` selects the same provider explicitly; `disabled` preserves the earlier underlying-only research phase. This stage does not append to production `signalRows`, evaluate a new strategy, reserve a position or submit an order. Independent inventory remains distinct from the existing primary-signal debit-vertical strategy.

For up to 12 shortlisted durable underlying identities, the runner requests every returned expiration date using `includeAllRoots=true`. Metadata is cached for one hour, scoped to token/environment/asset ID/provider symbol, and refreshed on a new exchange date. Failed refreshes never reuse expired metadata. The complete date list, including long-dated expirations, is retained; the default **research** chain window is 1–45 calendar days. Past/window-excluded dates and budget-deferred dates are separately recorded. This window does not change production DTE requirements.

The default chain budget is two expirations per selected underlying. The nearest eligible expiry is retained; additional eligible expirations rotate through a persisted cursor. Requests are round-robin across underlyings, so one symbol does not consume all the chain budget. Every returned strike/call/put is retained for fetched chains, without a contract-row slice. The provider endpoints do not document pagination; an unexpected pagination marker causes rejection instead of claiming the first page is complete. A complete response does not prove coverage beyond the provider response or that every listed contract is broker-tradable.

The parser cross-checks OCC symbol components against the provider's underlying, root, expiry, type and strike. Share-class symbols use the documented Tradier slash versus option-root dot mapping. Duplicate identities, missing/mismatched fields, adjusted roots and unknown/nonstandard multipliers stay in the archive with reasons; none is guessed into an executable contract. `verified_standard_contract` means those identity checks passed, not brokerage approval, liquidity approval or validation of a trade. Provider expiration type is retained when supplied; weekly/monthly labels are not inferred from dates. Greeks are not requested by inventory.

Bid/ask timestamps, age, side skew, volume and open interest remain descriptive research fields. Sandbox quotes are always labelled delayed, even if their supplied timestamps look recent. Current production quotes are labelled fresh research and still carry `eligibleForPaper=false`. Missing/stale/future/crossed data is preserved with reasons. No quote cache is reused as a current observation. Verified contract-member additions/removals detect newly observed strikes and missing members; identity failures suppress removal claims. These are provider observations, not authoritative delisting events.

The token pacer is shared with the existing quote adapter. Research preserves five requests of reported quota headroom, stops after auth/quota failures, and uses a 45-second request-start budget (configurable to at most 60 seconds). Busy queues/long cooldowns defer research without clearing cooldown state or waiting indefinitely. Requests have bounded timeouts/retries; this is not a guarantee that the entire scanner finishes within the research budget. Cross-process/account-wide quotas remain unfinished.

This standalone paper-only bot consumes an intraday directional signal and a synchronized equity/ETF option chain. Shared-journal mode accepts fresh delivered Stocks and ETF/ETP signals in the explicit scalp, momentum, session, or intraday/day-trade strategy family; swing/long-hold and unknown strategy labels are excluded. Other primary-bot inputs can be supplied manually, but their session, catalyst, quote, and strategy checks remain independently required. The initial strategy selects a liquid same-expiry call debit vertical for a long underlying signal or a put debit vertical for a short signal. It requires positive defined-risk geometry, open interest and volume, bounded individual-leg spreads, fresh quotes synchronized to the underlying signal and pairwise within the configured maximum leg skew, and an expiry inside the configured DTE window. Tradier option-leg quote timestamps more than five seconds ahead of the local scan clock are rejected; small clock tolerance is not proof of source-clock accuracy.

Numeric underlying and chain fields are accepted only as finite numbers or nonblank numeric strings. Boolean/blank prices, strikes, multipliers, open interest, volume, and Greek values cannot be coerced into market measurements; invalid chain legs are reported as rejected and incomplete Greeks remain unavailable.

The paper plan sets a premium stop at 50% of debit and a target at 2× debit capped at the spread width, then requires closure before the underlying session ends. The shared risk gate sizes by the vertical's full maximum debit loss (plus fees/slippage reserves), not merely the premium stop distance. Options positions are keyed to the underlying for cross-bot conflict detection. Reserved orders retain both OCC leg symbols; in `OPTIONS_SOURCE=tradier` production mode, the runner requests fresh, synchronized leg quotes for existing shared-ledger positions and forms a conservative liquidation bid from long-leg bid minus short-leg ask, floored at zero because a debit vertical cannot have negative value. The monitor verifies the live Tradier market clock, all four bid/ask timestamps, their session date, and maximum leg skew; it never calls an order endpoint. Sandbox, missing credentials, invalid legacy metadata, stale quotes, and unavailable quotes do not fabricate marks. When Tradier returns Greeks, the selected spread records available leg delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho, IV, and each provider update timestamp. These are descriptive only; they do not change approval, sizing, exits, or simulated P&L.

Strategy overrides may tighten the default gates but cannot loosen them: minimum open interest 100, volume 1, maximum individual-leg spread 15%, option/underlying quote age 10 seconds, pairwise leg skew 3 seconds, catalyst review age 30 minutes, assignment review age 24 hours, maximum DTE 45, minimum reward/risk 1.5, and pre-close exit buffer at least 5 minutes. Alpaca endpoint and event schemas: [Corporate Actions API](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1) and [official API schema](https://github.com/alpacahq/cli/blob/main/api/specs/market-data-api.json).

## Offline chronological replay

`npm run backtest -- <dataset.json>` replays one **fixed** vertical contract pair per run. The input includes `underlyingSymbol`, ordered `spreadSymbols` as `[longLegSymbol, shortLegSymbol]`, `accountEquityUsd`, strategy `config`, shared-risk `policy`, and strictly increasing `observations`. Each observation supplies `asOf`, a composite executable quote `{ bid, ask, time, quality: "executable" }`, and context with the underlying `signal` and point-in-time `chain` snapshot (including both leg quotes). Composite spread bid/ask must match leg-derived quotes and candidate timestamps. The command does not call Tradier or other providers. Synthetic tests validate mechanics only; no licensed historical option-chain replay, IV/Greeks-based lifecycle, assignment model, or profitability evidence is included.

Fees and slippage inputs are per contract for a complete round trip; the bot reserves twice each amount per vertical because it contains two option legs. Configure conservative values from the intended venue/account schedule; blank values prevent strategy approval. Options paper candidates force an exit before the verified regular-session close. OPTIONS_FORCE_EXIT_BUFFER_MINUTES defaults to 5 and is limited to 5–30 minutes; new entries are rejected once the buffer begins. This reduces overnight exposure but does not model broker exercise or assignment events. Offline replay datasets for call spreads must carry the same point-in-time `signal.optionAssignmentReview` object used by the production strategy.

Optional scheduled-event screening: set `OPTIONS_CATALYST_SOURCE=finnhub` and `FINNHUB_API_KEY` to request the shared three-date US economic-event window. Medium/high-impact events, missing credentials, malformed data, or provider failure block catalyst clearance before option quotes or chains are requested. The feed is Premium and remains off by default. Set `OPTIONS_CATALYST_SOURCE=alpaca` with read-only Alpaca data keys to inspect the prior 30 minutes of company headlines for each signal underlying, or set `OPTIONS_CATALYST_SOURCE=finnhub+alpaca` to run both checks in sequence. A news item whose Alpaca `created_at` or `updated_at` is later than the earlier review invalidates it; these timestamps are retained separately and the latest activity time controls re-review. Malformed/incomplete coverage blocks entries. Headlines are retained with publisher and validated HTTPS source URL when supplied, but are not interpreted for sentiment or direction. This uses no order endpoint. Neither source replaces a fresh source-backed review or validates option-specific event risk.
